# Defer independent reference data for ingredient allergen interpretation

Life Goods presents Open Food Facts data without adopting it as its own catalog or verification authority. Independent reference data remains a useful future direction because comparing Open Food Facts allergen tags with evidence derived from Open Food Facts taxonomies shares source lineage. It is not adopted by the current implementation: the current matcher deliberately retains the Open Food Facts taxonomy, and no Codex/FoodOn/FSA mapping bundle is wired into production. The research and proposed contract below remain context for a future decision; unrecognized ingredients remain Source Data Unavailable rather than proof that a Product is allergen-free.
