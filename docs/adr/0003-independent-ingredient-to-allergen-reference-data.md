# Adopt independent reference data for ingredient allergen interpretation

Life Goods presents Open Food Facts data without adopting it as its own catalog or verification authority. Comparing Open Food Facts allergen tags with evidence derived from Open Food Facts taxonomies creates a circular verification loop that shares source lineage. To provide genuine independent evidence, Life Goods decouples allergen category policy from detection coverage, adopting the Codex Alimentarius CXS 1-1985 (Amended 2026) standard as the category authority, FoodOn and UK Food Standards Agency concepts for stable machine-readable identifiers, and a project-curated, hash-pinned reference bundle with cited relationship semantics. Open Food Facts is preserved as the unchanged source dataset for Product records and as a labeled baseline comparator, while unrecognized ingredients remain Source Data Unavailable rather than proof that a Product is allergen-free.
