# Keep legal and authenticity determinations outside the platform

The platform has no authority to determine that a package is illegal, fraudulent, or counterfeit. Any future reporting feature will hand the shopper to a reviewed Official Channel without submitting or tracking the complaint, and any future Brand Authenticity Advisory will require an authenticated, authorized brand owner while remaining educational rather than a platform verdict. Both capabilities are deferred beyond the first pilot; the boundary remains accepted so later work does not recreate an internal adjudication system.
