# Ship external Product evidence before the reviewed local catalog

MVP-1 sources Product records exclusively from a project-hosted Active OFF Dataset Version and defers locally observed Product seed data, reviewed-local precedence, Product Claims, Preferred Claims, conflicts, and catalog moderation until after MVP-1. MVP-1 may still produce narrowly scoped assessments from separately versioned, human-reviewed reference datasets hosted in PostgreSQL; approving those reference releases does not review or verify an OFF Product record. This trades local-market Product coverage and Khmer ingredient explanations for an earlier, operationally controlled release with honest citations, English-first explanations, explicit unknowns, and no fabricated reviewed catalog.
